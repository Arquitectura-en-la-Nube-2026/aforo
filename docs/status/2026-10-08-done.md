## Found on `dev` and fixed (worth a line in the artifact)

- **Orders did not boot in its image** (`#38`): a dependency installed only through another
  service on our machines. Every local test passed; only the deploy showed it.
- **Every payment webhook became a 500 at the gateway** (`#39`): the payment still completed
  because the webhook is idempotent, but Cloud Tasks retried each notification five times.
- **The first queue join after a pause failed** (`#40`): scale to zero, a ~3.2 s cold start and a
  2.5 s gateway timeout. The prewarmer only covers sales windows.

These are concrete examples of why the idempotent consumer and the blue/green smoke test exist:
the duplicate webhooks changed nothing, and the broken orders revision never got traffic.

## Decisions taken

- **ADR-0007** (Oct 7): the web is Next.js.
- **Platform took over** catalog, inventory and finishing orders, because their owners were not
  responsive.
- **Catalog behavior the contract left open** (catalog README): publishing twice emits nothing
  the second time; a list without `status` includes drafts; no organizer roles (D-06, D-08).
- **Seed dates are fixed**, so the prewarmer skips the seeded events and the seed never raises
  min instances.
- **Timeouts above the 2.5 s convention** in the gateway: orders 15 s (it waits for its own E-06
  retries), mock pages 10 s, queue 8 s (cold start).
- **Always open the web at** `https://aforo-web-68220265740.southamerica-east1.run.app` (CORS).
