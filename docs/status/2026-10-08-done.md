## Found on `dev` (worth a line in the artifact)

- **Orders did not boot in its image** (`#38`): a dependency installed only through another
  service on our machines. The revision never got traffic.
- **Every payment webhook became a 500 at the gateway** (`#39`): Cloud Tasks retried each
  notification five times; the idempotent consumer absorbed all of them (one `OrderPaid`).
- **The first queue join after a pause failed** (`#40`): ~3.2 s cold start vs the gateway's 2.5 s
  upstream timeout. The prewarmer only covers sales windows.
- **A spike of 200 simultaneous reservations misses the 300 ms p95** on one inventory instance with
  2 connections (808 ms); metered by the waiting room it is 75 ms. Argument for the queue.
- **The circuit breaker re-opened once on recovery**: its half-open probe hit the mock's new
  revision on a cold instance (2.8 s > 2 s timeout). Correct behavior; for the demo, warm the mock.
- **The alert closes 30 min later, not at once**: a log-based count has no data when idle, so
  `auto_close` ends the incident.
- **The demo runbook's minimum-instances command read the wrong field** (fixed).
- **`make deploy` can watch the previous run** if GitHub has not listed the new one after 5 s
  (not fixed; workaround in the runbook).

## Decisions taken

- **ADR-0007** (Oct 7): the web is Next.js.
- **Platform took over** catalog, inventory and finishing orders.
- **Catalog behavior the contract left open** (catalog README): publishing twice emits nothing
  the second time; a list without `status` includes drafts; no organizer roles (D-06, D-08).
- **Seed dates are fixed**, so the prewarmer skips the seeded events.
- **Gateway timeouts** per upstream: catalog and inventory 2.5 s (the convention in
  `contracts/conventions.md` says 2 s), orders 15 s, mock pages 10 s, queue 8 s.
- **E-02 on `dev` runs through the gateway** (as a buyer), not against inventory directly: no
  service-account token needed, and it exercises the real path. It skips the waiting room because
  the pass is not verified (D-07).
